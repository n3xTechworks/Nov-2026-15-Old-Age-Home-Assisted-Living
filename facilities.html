Spaces made for comfortable, unhurried days

Garden-view suites, a sunlit dining room, two acres of walled gardens and quiet corners to rest, all step-free and close to help.

Suites Dining Gardens Activities Wellness What is included

Three kinds of suite, all with a garden view

Every room is private, step-free and en-suite, with space for the armchair, the photographs and the quilt that make it feel like home.

Prices include all meals, laundry, activities and personal care. Nursing and memory care are priced after a free assessment.

Dining

The Orchard Dining Room and the Tea Room

Marco and his kitchen team cook everything fresh, using vegetables from our own kitchen garden. Meals are served at small tables of four, and the Tea Room is open all afternoon for family and friends.

  • Three meals a day plus afternoon tea with home baking
  • Soft, diabetic and cultural diets cooked with the same care
  • Breakfast whenever you wake, in the dining room or your suite
  • Family are welcome to join for lunch at no charge

Gardens & courtyard

Two acres of walled gardens, all step-free

The old orchard walls keep the wind out and the sun in. Level brick paths loop past rose beds, a kitchen garden and a sheltered courtyard, with a bench every few minutes of walking.

  • Raised beds at chair height for the Kitchen Garden Club
  • A covered courtyard for fresh air on rainy days
  • Handrails and lighting along every path
  • Garden doors on the latch, so residents come and go freely

Recreation

Rooms for hobbies, reading and good company

Our activities team runs more than twenty sessions a week, but these rooms are open all day for anyone who simply wants to potter.

Craft studio

The Craft Studio

Watercolours, knitting, flower pressing and card making around one long table, with big windows for good light.

  • Classes Mon, Wed & Fri
Library

The Library

Two thousand books, large-print titles, audiobooks and the daily papers, with a fortnightly visit from the town library van.

  • Large print & audio
Games lounge

The Games Lounge

Cards, dominoes, jigsaws and the Thursday quiz, plus a big screen for films, matches and the occasional royal wedding.

  • Quiz night Thursdays

Wellness

Places to stay strong, feel cared for and rest

Gentle exercise, a little pampering and somewhere quiet to sit all help a day feel good. Everything here is on the ground floor, a short walk from the suites.

Therapy pool

Hydrotherapy Pool

Warm water at 33°C, a hoist for easy entry and guided sessions with our physiotherapist to ease stiff joints.

  • Hoist access
Fitness & physio

Fitness and Physio Studio

Treadmills with handrails, seated bikes and parallel bars for one-to-one physiotherapy and steady-legs classes.

  • Physio 5 days a week
Salon

Hair Salon and Barber

Our visiting stylist sets, cuts and trims twice a week, and a manicure and hand massage is never far away.

  • Tuesdays & Fridays

The Quiet Room

A peaceful room with garden windows for prayer, reflection or a moment alone. Local faith leaders of every tradition visit on request, and a Sunday service is held each week.

Everything included

One weekly fee, with no surprise extras

Your fee covers the suite and everything below. The only extras are hairdressing, newspapers and outings with a ticket price.

  • All meals, snacks and afternoon tea
  • Daily housekeeping and weekly deep clean
  • Laundry and ironing, labelled for you
  • Fast Wi-Fi and a phone line in every suite
  • Flat-screen TV with all licences paid
  • Twenty or more activities every week
  • Garden, library, pool and studio access
  • Nurse call system at every bed and bathroom
  • Minibus trips into Stamford twice a week
  • Guest meals and overnight room for family
