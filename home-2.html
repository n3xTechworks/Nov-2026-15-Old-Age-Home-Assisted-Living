A day at Thymewood

Days that follow your mum, not a timetable

There is a gentle rhythm to life here, but nobody is woken for breakfast or hurried to bed. This is how a typical Tuesday unfolds.

Morning, at your own pace

Breakfast is served until half past ten, in your suite or the dining room. Carers help with washing and dressing exactly as each resident likes it.

  • 7:30 to 10:30

Midday, lunch together

A two-course lunch cooked from the kitchen garden. Families are welcome to book a seat at the table, and Sunday roast is always a full house.

  • 12:30 to 14:00

Afternoon, something to look forward to

Choir, painting, a walk round the rose beds or a quiet nap. Afternoon tea arrives at half past three, with cake baked that morning.

  • 14:00 to 17:00

Evening, winding down

A light supper, a film or a game of cards in the lounge, then a warm drink in bed. Night nurses check in as quietly as each person prefers.

  • 17:30 onwards
