Questions families ask

Honest answers before you visit

If your question is not here, ask Daniel on the day, or call us any time.

Visiting

Can I visit at any time?

Families and friends are welcome every day from 9:00 to 20:00, with no need to book. We only ask that you avoid the dining room at mealtimes unless you are joining for lunch.

Can we come for a meal or bring the dog?

Yes to both. Guests can join any meal for a small charge, and well-behaved dogs on a lead are very welcome in the lounges and gardens.

How long does a show-round visit take?

Most visits take about an hour, including tea. Stay as long as you like: there is no rush and no sales pitch.

Fees & funding

What is included in the weekly fee?

Your room, all meals and snacks, laundry, housekeeping, activities and outings, Wi-Fi and the personal care set out in your care plan. Hairdressing and private chiropody are extra.

Do you accept council-funded residents?

Yes. We work with the local authority and can explain how a needs assessment and any top-up payment would work in your situation.

Will the fees go up every year?

Fees are reviewed once a year in April. We give at least 28 days' written notice and explain any change in plain terms.

Moving in

Can my mum bring her own furniture?

Please do. Favourite chairs, pictures, a bookcase and bedding all help a room feel like home. Our handyman will hang pictures on the day.

How quickly can someone move in?

Once our nurse has completed an assessment and a room is free, a move can often happen within a week. Respite stays can sometimes be arranged sooner.

Can we try a short stay first?

Yes. A respite stay of two weeks or more is a gentle way to get to know us, and many residents later choose to stay on.
