Nursing care

Registered nurses, day and night

Our nurses know each resident's history, so changes are noticed early and handled calmly. A local GP visits every Wednesday, and we arrange dentists, opticians and chiropodists on site.

What is included

  • Medicines managed and given by nurses
  • Wound, diabetes and catheter care
  • Weekly GP round and hospital liaison

Who it is for: people living with long-term conditions, frailty or recovery needs who want a nurse close by.

Memory care

Gentle support for memory loss

The Willow wing is a calm, homely space for sixteen people living with dementia. Colour-coded doors, a circular garden walk and familiar routines help each day feel safe and recognisable.

What is included

  • Dementia-trained carers on every shift
  • Life-story work and memory boxes
  • Music, sensory and reminiscence sessions

Who it is for: people with Alzheimer's or other dementias who need patience, structure and a secure garden.

Daily living support

Help with the everyday, at your own pace

Each resident has a named key carer who learns how they like to start the day. We help with as much or as little as is wanted, and step back as confidence returns.

What is included

  • Washing, bathing and dressing support
  • Help to meals, the garden and activities
  • Laundry, housekeeping and appointments

Who it is for: people who are mostly independent but find some parts of the day harder than they used to.

Therapy & mobility

Physiotherapy that keeps people steady

Our in-house physiotherapist sees residents one to one and runs chair exercise and balance classes. After a fall or a hospital stay, we set clear goals and celebrate each step back.

What is included

  • Mobility assessment within the first week
  • One-to-one physio and hydrotherapy
  • Falls prevention and walking aids fitted

Who it is for: anyone recovering from illness, surgery or a fall, or keen to stay strong and confident on their feet.

Respite stays

Short stays that give everyone a rest

A respite stay is a full Thymewood welcome for a few weeks: a furnished suite, the same care and every activity, while family carers recharge. Many guests treat it as a gentle trial before moving in.

What is included

  • Furnished suite, meals and all activities
  • Care plan agreed before arrival
  • Stays from two weeks, booked ahead

Who it is for: people whose family carer needs a break, or who are home from hospital and not quite ready to be alone.

How care begins

Five unhurried steps from first call to settled in

Nothing is rushed. Most families take two to four weeks from the first visit to moving day, and we are with you at every stage.

  1. I

    A free care assessment

    Our Head of Nursing visits your parent at home or in hospital to listen, ask questions and understand what matters to them.

  2. II

    A care plan written together

    We write the plan with your parent and with you: routines, health needs, food, faith and the little things that make a day good.

  3. III

    A gentle move-in day

    The suite is ready with their own things in place, the key carer is there to greet them, and family are welcome to stay for supper.

  4. IV

    Reviews at six weeks, then monthly

    We sit down together to see what is working and change the plan as needs change, never waiting for a problem.

  5. V

    Regular updates for family

    A monthly letter from the key carer, a call whenever you want one and an open door for lunch, so you always know how they are.
