Welcome to Thymewood

A place where your parent can still feel completely like themselves

We are a family-run residence for 64 people, set in an old walled garden on the edge of Stamford. Since 1994 we have kept things simple: small teams who stay, real home cooking, and days that follow each resident rather than a timetable.

  • Your own suite, furnished with the things you love
  • The same small team of carers, week after week
  • Open visiting for family, friends and well-behaved dogs

Our promises

Four quiet promises behind every day

Choosing care for someone you love is never simple. These are the commitments we make on the first day, and the ones families tell us matter most.

  1. I

    Dignity before routine

    Your parent chooses when to wake, what to wear and how to spend the day. We knock, we ask, and we use the name they prefer.

  2. II

    A named carer who knows them

    The same small team each week, so faces become familiar, little habits are remembered and trust has time to grow.

  3. III

    Nature every single day

    Step-free paths, raised beds at chair height and a garden walk offered to everyone, in any weather, with a blanket if needed.

  4. IV

    Honest updates for families

    A monthly letter from the key carer, a call whenever you want one, and the door always open for lunch.

Care services

The right help, gently given

Some residents need a hand in the mornings; others need nursing around the clock. Care plans are written with your parent, reviewed with you, and change as needs change.

Nursing care

Registered nurses, day and night

Medication, wound care and long-term conditions handled calmly by nurses who know your parent's history, with a weekly GP round.
