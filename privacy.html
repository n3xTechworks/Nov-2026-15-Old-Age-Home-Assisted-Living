Privacy Policy

How Thymewood would collect, use and protect the details you share with us, in plain words.

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Who we are

Thymewood Garden Residence ("Thymewood", "we", "us") is a fictional care residence created for this website demonstration. This policy explains, in plain terms, how we would handle personal information if Thymewood were real. No data entered on this site is stored, transmitted or shared anywhere — every form here is for demonstration only.

What we would collect

If you booked a visit, joined our garden letter or contacted us, we would collect the details you provide: your name, email, phone number, and any message or preferences you add, such as a preferred visiting date or area of interest.

How we would use it

  • To arrange and confirm a visit or enquiry you have asked for.
  • To send the monthly garden letter, only to people who have asked for it.
  • To answer questions about care, fees or moving in.

We would never sell your details, and we would only share them with a third party where the law requires it or you have asked us to, such as passing a referral to your GP with your permission.

Keeping information safe

We would keep personal information only for as long as it is needed, store it securely, and let you ask to see, correct or delete it at any time.

Cookies

This demonstration site remembers your theme and layout choice (light or dark, left-to-right or right-to-left) in your browser's local storage, on this device only. It is not used to track you, and no analytics or advertising cookies are set.

Contact us

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@thymewood.example or by using our contact form.
